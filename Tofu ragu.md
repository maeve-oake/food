# Tofu ragu

- serves 5
- 1 hour 30 mins

## ingredients

- 200g extra-firm tofu
- 350g twizzly pasta of your choice
- 1 carrot
- 2 celery sticks
- 1 red onion
- 2 tins plum tomatoes
- 4 cloves garlic
- paprika
- various quantities of green leafy herbs
- sugar, salt, pepper
- soy sauce
- tomato puree
- olive oil
- balsamic vinegar
- milk (preferably oat)
- vegetable stock cube
- parmesan cheese

## prep tofu

1. drain the tofu packets and remove the blocks
2. press the tofu gently between two paper towels
3. replace the paper towels and repeat until the towels aren't soaked

make sure you don't crush or crumble the tofu!

## prep veg

1. finely chop carrot
2. finely chop celery
3. finely chop red onion
4. finely chop garlic

## cook sauce

1. add olive oil into a large frying pan
2. add all the veg and fry off until slightly browned
3. add a good squeeze of tomato puree and fry off again
4. add paprika and herbs, then fry off again
5. add a splash of balsamic vinegar to deglaze the pan
6. add both tins of tomatoes, and rinse the tins into the pan
7. crush the larger tomatoes with your spoon
8. crumble the vegetable stock cube and stir in
9. add a splash of soy sauce
10. add lots of salt and pepper
11. add 1 tsp of sugar

let simmer while you cook the tofu.

## cook tofu

1. grate the dried tofu block onto a lined baking tray 
2. drizzle with olive oil and soy sauce
3. bake for about 20 minutes, shaking the tray to flip the tofu regularly

now is a good time to boil the pasta!

## combine

the tofu is ready when its a golden colour and SLIGHTLY crispy

1. mix the tofu into the sauce
2. mix the pasta into the sauce
3. add a splash of milk

## serve

serve with fresh parsley and vegan parmesan

### enjoy ^_^
